      How to check a photo

      1. Upload your photo, or paste it from your clipboard.
      2. Choose what it is for, or enter the numbers from your form under Custom.
      3. Read the results. Each check shows what your photo has and what is needed.
      4. If something fails, resize the photo and download the fixed copy.

      What each check means

      Dimensions
      The width and height of the photo in pixels.
      File size
      How much space the file takes up. Uploads often have a limit such as 100 KB or 2 MB. Here 1 KB is 1,024 bytes.
      Format
      The type of file, such as JPEG, PNG or WebP.
      Aspect ratio
      The shape of the photo: width compared with height. Square is 1:1 and a typical phone photo is 4:3.
      Resolution
      The total pixel count in megapixels, plus the DPI value stored in the file if there is one. DPI is only a label. The pixel size is what usually decides whether a photo is accepted.

      Questions

      Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

      No. The checking and resizing run inside your browser. The photo stays on your device.

      Will resizing stretch my photo?

      No. If the new shape is different, the photo is cropped to fit. You can move the kept area before you resize.

      How do I get a photo under a file size limit?

      Type the limit in the "Keep under (KB)" box and save as JPEG or WebP. The tool lowers the quality just enough to fit. If it still can't fit, make the photo smaller in pixels.

      Are the built-in requirements official?

      They are common published values, but rules change and differ between offices and websites. Always confirm with whoever asked for the photo, or use Custom to enter their exact numbers.

      My iPhone photo won't open. Why?

      Some browsers can't read HEIC photos. Change the camera format to "Most Compatible" or convert the photo to JPEG first.
